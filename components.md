📦 Components Used – GREEN CIRCUIT
🧠 Controller

ESP32 Development Board
Used for sensor data processing, edge AI logic, and device control

🌡️ Sensors

Soil Moisture Sensor (Analog)
Measures soil water content to determine irrigation needs

DHT11 Temperature & Humidity Sensor
Monitors ambient temperature and humidity conditions

Rain Sensor Module (Analog)
Detects rainfall to prevent unnecessary irrigation

📟 Display

0.96” OLED Display (I2C)
Displays real-time sensor data and system status

⚙️ Actuation

Relay Module (5V) / Motor Driver
Controls the water pump automatically

Mini Submersible Water Pump
Used for irrigation control

🔌 Power & Accessories

Power Supply (Battery / Adapter / Solar – optional)

Jumper Wires (Male–Male, Male–Female)

Breadboard / PCB

✅ Notes

All components are low-cost and easily available
